## ⚖ The Bootstrap Constraint

**No spend before revenue.** Adi's explicit constraint, 2026-05-11.

This reorders the authority playbook from "spend to build credibility" to **"earn first, spend second."** Every action below is tagged:

- 🆓 **Free** — founder time + existing assets (no cash outlay)
- 💰 **Paid** — requires spend, **deferred** until Phase 1 hits revenue target

האסטרטגיה הופכת ל-3-phase:
1. **Phase 0 — Ship** (engineering, no marketing cost)
2. **Phase 1 — Lifetime Deal for 100 Founding Members** (revenue spike, all 🆓 outreach)
3. **Phase 2 — Free Authority Build** (parallel to Phase 1 — LinkedIn voice + PM community + philosophy page + advisor outreach via equity-not-cash)
4. **Phase 3 — Paid Authority** (only unlocked after Phase 1 revenue: $10k+ minimum)

## Phase 1 — Lifetime Deal for 100 Founding Members 🎯

**Goal:** $10k–15k in 4–8 weeks. Funds Phase 3 spend. Builds first 100 testimonials + community.

### 1.1 The Offer

**"Founding 100"** — paid lifetime access, capped at 100 sales.

**Pricing options (Adi decides):**
- **$99** anchor (~ 11 months of $9/mo Family Plan)
- **$129** anchor (~ 14 months — slightly stretched but still feels like a deal)
- **$149** anchor (~ 16 months — highest comfortable ceiling)

**My recommendation: $99** for the first 50, **$149** for the next 50. Built-in scarcity + price-anchored social proof ("the first 50 spots are gone in 2 weeks").

**What they get (lifetime, forever):**
- Family Plan equivalent — 3 kids, unlimited tasks, full features
- "Founding Member" badge inside the app (cheap to ship, valued by buyers)
- Priority feedback channel — direct DM/email to Adi
- All future features free
- Locked-in pricing forever (immunity to future price hikes)

**What WE get:**
- $10k–15k revenue
- 100 paying-customer testimonials
- 100 highly-engaged users (= retention case studies)
- Word-of-mouth seeds in ADHD parenting communities

### 1.2 Outreach Sources (all 🆓)

| Source | Estimated reach | Conversion expectation | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| 47 Lovable POC users (existing emails) | 47 | ~20% = ~9 sales | Low — one direct email |

| ADHD parenting Facebook groups (Hebrew, Israel) | ~3 groups | ~10–15 sales | Medium |
| Reddit r/ADHD_parenting + r/ADHD | ~300k members | ~10–15 sales | Medium — long-form replies |

**Total realistic target:** 80–110 sales — covers the 100 goal with room for skew.
