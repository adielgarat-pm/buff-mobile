## The Gemini pass (2026-07-27, Gemini 3.1 Pro, goal-explicit)

Prompt stated the three measurable goals (open the app and do one thing today · repeat use within
a week · join the WhatsApp group) and asked for a sharp critique, not compliments.

### Adopted

| Gemini's point | Why it was right |
|---|---|
| **Three features is too many; focus on one.** | It converges with our own metric — the north star for this feature is Repeat Capture Rate, not "awareness of three things". v2 leads with Smart Organizer and one CTA. **Modified, not obeyed:** the other two are compressed into a single italic line rather than deleted. This is an announcement to an existing base who have seen none of them; discovery still has value, it just must not compete for the tap. |
| **"ופעולה קטנה אחת להיום" is a guilt trigger.** | The sharpest catch. To an exhausted parent, "one small action for today" is another chore — and it fails our own Pillar 2 (no pressure framing). Removed; the AI coach is now described by what it gives, not what she must do. |
| **"מחקתי יום, מילאתי פעם אחת, העתקתי" reads as admin work.** | Correct, and it broke our own rule (`feedback_marketing_why_what` — outcome, not mechanism). Replaced with the outcome: the schedule swaps when the season changes and the gear reaches the child. |
| **Subject line is long and reads promotional.** | Naming the product in the subject of a founder-personal email suppresses opens. v2 subjects are pain-first and product-free; #1 keeps the concrete "12 rows" hook that was the strongest thing in v1. |
| **Opening as shared pain, not solved problem.** | Warmer entry. Adopted in spirit ("הראש שלי כבר לא פנוי להחזיק את זה"), in Adi's voice rather than Gemini's wording. |

### Rejected, with reasons

| Gemini's point | Why we kept ours |
|---|---|
| **"הפסקתי להעתיק דברים ביד" is condescending** — reads as "I've moved on, you're still doing it wrong". | Misreads founder voice. It is first-person about her own pain, not a comparison to the reader. The phrase is gone from v2 for a different reason (structure), not because the critique held. |
| **The community CTA cannibalizes the primary CTA — move it after.** | It already *was* after it. In an email a secondary link below the primary CTA does not compete for the same decision; both can be taken. Kept last. |
| **Its rewritten WhatsApp message opens "היי {{שם}}".** | Directly contradicts the brief — the WhatsApp message is **general, no personalization**. Do not paste Gemini's version. |
| **Its rewrite invented "קבוצות הגן".** | Wrong audience: BUFF is 6–18, not preschool. A reminder that its replacement copy needs reading, not copying. |

## Copy decisions (standing)

- **One hero feature, one CTA**, with the exact in-app path.
- **First person** — a founder's own usage outperforms a feature list.
- **Outcome before mechanism**; never "Gemini parses a file".
- **Reply request at the end** — lifts reply rate and produces the feedback you want anyway.
- **Community CTA last**, after the value has been delivered.

**Two examples to confirm before sending:** the camp-schedule photo → 12 items is recorded as a
verification that ran on Adi's phone (2026-07-24), and the school→camp timetable replacement is
recorded as Adi's own scenario (RELEASE_QUEUE, #323). Confirm both read as genuine personal use,
not as a QA run.

read-only blocks). After the send, that report answers "did the announcement move usage" directly.
Baseline recorded in #394: 5 runs, 2 families, **reach-through 0%** (15 transferred items, none
completed by a child yet).
