## Copy decisions (why it's written this way)

- **Smart Organizer leads** — the only feature with a visible payoff inside 10 seconds.
- **First person** ("I photographed…") — a founder's own usage outperforms a feature list.
- **Outcome before mechanism** (less in your head / your child sees what to pack), never "Gemini parses a file" — per `feedback_marketing_why_what`.
- **One CTA per piece**, with the exact in-app path.
- **Reply request at the end** — lifts reply rate and produces the feedback you want anyway.
- **Community CTA placed last**, after the value is delivered — asking to join before giving anything converts worse.

**Two examples to confirm before sending:** the camp-schedule photo → 12 items is recorded as a
verification that ran on Adi's phone (2026-07-24), and the school→camp timetable replacement is
recorded as Adi's own scenario (RELEASE_QUEUE, #323). Confirm both read as genuine personal use,
not as a QA run.

**Measurement:** PR #394 landed the usability instrumentation for this feature
(`scripts/capture-usage.sql`, 5 read-only blocks). After the send, that report answers
"did the announcement move usage" directly — Repeat Capture Rate is the north star.
Baseline recorded in #394: 5 runs, 2 families, **reach-through 0%** (15 transferred items,
none completed by a child yet).
