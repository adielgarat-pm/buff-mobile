# Track C — Problem interview with parents who have never heard of BUFF

**Who:** 8–10 parents of children aged 7–14 with ADHD (diagnosed or in process). Sources: ADHD-parent Facebook groups (IL + EN), r/ADHDparenting, school WhatsApp groups, one clinician/coach if reachable. Mix IL and US/UK.
**Format:** 20–25 minute call. **Do not mention BUFF until the very end, and even then only if they ask.** No demo, no landing page.
**Goal:** is the pain BUFF targets a top-2 weekly pain, have they *paid* to solve it, and would they believe an app could help.

---

## Recruiting post (no product mention)

**EN**
> I'm a mom of a teen with ADHD and I'm doing research (not selling anything) on what daily life really looks like for families like ours — mornings, homework, the stuff nobody sees. Looking for 8 parents of kids 7–14 for a 20-minute chat. Happy to share what I learn with the group afterwards.

**HE**
> אני אמא לנער עם ADHD ועושה מחקר (לא מוכרת כלום) על איך נראים באמת חיי היומיום של משפחות כמו שלנו: בקרים, שיעורי בית, כל מה שאף אחד לא רואה. מחפשת 8 הורים לילדים בגיל 7–14 לשיחה של 20 דקות. אשמח לשתף את הקבוצה במה שאלמד.

## Questions

| # | EN | HE | What we listen for |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Walk me through yesterday morning, from wake-up to the door. | תעברי איתי על הבוקר של אתמול, מהיקיצה עד הדלת. | Concrete friction points. Count the reminders. Who does what. |
| 2 | And the afternoon: homework, bag, screens, bedtime. Where does it get hard? | ואחר הצהריים: שיעורים, תיק, מסכים, שינה. איפה זה נהיה קשה? | Which of our target pains actually shows up, unprompted. |
| 3 | What is the single worst recurring moment of your week? | מה הרגע החוזר הכי גרוע בשבוע שלך? | The pain to rank. Is it in BUFF's scope at all? |
| 4 | What have you tried for it? Charts, apps, therapy, coaching, medication changes, anything. | מה ניסית בשביל זה? טבלאות, אפליקציות, טיפול, אימון, שינויי תרופות, כל דבר. | Solution history. Names of apps. Why each was dropped. |
| 5 | What did each of those cost you, in money and effort? Which ones did you actually pay for? | כמה כל אחד מהם עלה לך, בכסף ובמאמץ? על מה באמת שילמת? | Paid before = real WTP signal. Never paid = category may be "free or nothing". |
| 6 | If a friend told you "there's an app that helps with exactly this", what would you assume it does? Would you believe her? | אם חברה הייתה אומרת לך "יש אפליקציה שעוזרת בדיוק עם זה", מה היית מניחה שהיא עושה? היית מאמינה לה? | Category beliefs. Skepticism level. What "app for ADHD kids" means to them (screen-time worry, gamification cynicism, etc.). |
| 7 | Who would need to want it for it to work in your house: you, your child, both? | מי צריך לרצות את זה כדי שזה יעבוד אצלכם בבית: את, הילד/ה, שניכם? | Do parents already understand that the child is the user? This directly tests the handoff assumption. |
| 8 *(added 2026-09-17)* | After a bad morning — what happens in the car, or in the ten minutes after? What does your child do with it later? | אחרי בוקר גרוע — מה קורה באוטו, או בעשר הדקות אחרי? מה הילד/ה עושה עם זה אחר כך? | The after-moment. If parents describe shame/replay loops unprompted, the Pillar 2 positioning ("being late isn't what hurts") has a live audience. See research digest §4.1, §5.1. |

**Closing (only if asked what this is for):** "I'm building something in this space and I'm trying to find out whether it's worth building before I go further." Offer to send the summary. Don't pitch.
