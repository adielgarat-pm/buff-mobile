## What this email does

Three jobs in one short letter:

1. **Reconnect** — these people signed up for BUFF on Lovable weeks/months ago. Many haven't heard from us since. Tell them what changed and why.
2. **Make the offer** — lifetime free access for the founding cohort, in exchange for using BUFF on real school days through 2026-06-30 and giving feedback.
3. **Set expectations honestly** — what's in beta, what's missing, what's coming. No marketing puff.

**Tone reference:** BUFF_MESSAGING §8.6 — *"Personal-letter from founder. Adi's voice. Plain text > HTML."*

**This is not a marketing email.** It's a founder writing to people who already said yes once.

## What I deliberately didn't put in

- ❌ **No price.** Lifetime free for this cohort. Mentioning the $9/mo Family Plan would muddy the gift.
- ❌ **No feature list.** Lead with WHY/WHAT (per Adi's memory: marketing copy: WHY/WHAT not HOW). The bullets above are *honesty about what's missing*, not a feature pitch.
- ❌ **No urgency framing.** No "limited spots" / "respond by Friday". The cohort is fixed at 49. Fake scarcity violates Spaceship Test.
- ❌ **No "we know your time is valuable."** Generic-empathy phrase. They know we know.
- ❌ **No Joon comparison.** Wrong audience — this list already chose BUFF once.
- ❌ **No screenshots.** Plain text only per BUFF_MESSAGING §8.6.
- ❌ **No long origin story.** They get the "mom of a teen" framing in 7 words: *"Built by a mom of a teen with ADHD."* The full founder story belongs on the landing page, not in this email.
- ❌ **No Play Store link in the body.** Internal testing links are personalized; goes out as the second touch after they reply "yes."
