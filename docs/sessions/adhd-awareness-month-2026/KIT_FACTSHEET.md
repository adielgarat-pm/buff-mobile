## The 5-line factsheet (paste into every email; do not add claims)

```
BUFF — ADHD routines & motivation app for families, ages 6–18
• Built with families: founded by a product manager who is mom of a teen with ADHD; the teen interface was co-designed with a 15-year-old with ADHD.
• Real rewards the child chooses (a movie night, a concert, an outing) instead of virtual coins or a pet to feed.
• A small, absolute win: about 3 tasks done = an active day — not 100%, not a percentage.
• Two interfaces: Buddy Mode (6–12) and Teen Mode (13–18), so the app follows the child through the years when ADHD makes daily life hardest.

Platforms: Android (Google Play) + web; iOS planned. 183 beta families to date. Press kit: buffadhd.com/press
```
