# SPEC — adhd-awareness-month-2026

## Problem
October is ADHD Awareness Month — the one month a year when ADDitude, Understood, podcasters and clinicians actively look for parent voices and refresh "best ADHD apps" roundups. Short-lead editors lock ~2–3 weeks out (≈ Sept 15). BUFF has a full messaging corpus but no press page, no approved bio, and has never been pitched for inclusion anywhere. (Source: agency review panel, PR lens — `docs/sessions/marketing-scout/REVIEW.md`.)
