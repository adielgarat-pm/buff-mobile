## למה המסמך הזה קיים

Three problems this doc solves:

1. **"5 posts isn't enough"** — Adi's correction 2026-05-13. Real SEO compounds with topical depth (Google rewards sites that own a topic). 5 posts = launch minimum, not strategy.
2. **"Where do posts come from?"** — bootstrap constraint per [GO_TO_MARKET](BUFF_GO_TO_MARKET.md). Writing 30 posts from scratch = ~100 hours. Repurposing existing brand-family docs = ~60 hours, with stronger consistency.
3. **"What ranks for what?"** — without keyword × persona mapping per post, the blog becomes a random pile. With it: a content engine.

This doc is the bridge between [BUFF_PERSONAS.md](BUFF_PERSONAS.md) (who's searching) + [BUFF_MESSAGING.md](BUFF_MESSAGING.md) / [BUFF_COMPETITORS.md](BUFF_COMPETITORS.md) (what we already know) + buffadhd.com/blog (where it lives).

## 1. Why a Blog (and Why Now)

| Lever | Why blog helps |
|---|---|
| **SEO** | Compounds free traffic over 6–18 months. P3 ("Tried Everything") and P2 ("Post-Diagnosis") parents Google before downloading apps. |
| **Authority** | Long-form content = trust signal for P5 (Coach-Curious). [PERSONAS §P5 trust signals](BUFF_PERSONAS.md): *"open documentation… is a brand asset for her."* |
| **Repurposing engine** | Same post = LinkedIn essay (Adi voice) + FB group reply (T-template form) + Reddit long reply + email newsletter. **One post produces 4 distribution moments.** |
| **Israeli market angle** | Hebrew SEO has lower competition. A Hebrew post ranking #1 in IL ADHD search has outsized impact relative to effort. |

**Risk to flag:** SEO is slow. Don't expect traffic for 6–12 weeks after first post. The bootstrap-friendly mistake is publishing 3 posts then stopping when traffic doesn't materialize. Compounding requires consistency.

## 3. Cadence Proposal

| Phase | Weeks | Posts | Pace |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Launch burst** | Weeks 1–2 | Posts #1, #8, #14, #23, #24 (5) | All within 14 days — Google needs ~5 to recognize a topical site |

| **Authority + story** | Weeks 15+ | Posts #25–31 + ongoing | 1/week |

**Why this order:** the launch burst hits one post from each major cluster, so Google indexes BUFF as covering the topical breadth. Then steady-state fills depth within each cluster.

## 4. Hebrew Adaptation Strategy

Pick the **10–12 highest-intent posts** (Clusters 1, 2 mostly) and translate. Rationale:
- Israeli ADHD search has lower competition — Hebrew posts can rank #1 fast (per [PERSONAS §5](BUFF_PERSONAS.md))
- 96% of beta is Israeli per PRD §4.3 — this audience is captive and underserved by Israeli content
- Hebrew translation should be **adapted, not literal** — per [BRAND §6](BUFF_BRAND.md) tone of voice in HE differs substantially from EN

**Recommended HE starter set:** posts #1 (Joon), #6 (teens), #8 (morning), #12 (sticker charts), #14 (post-diagnosis). 5 posts, biggest pain coverage.

## 7. Per-Post Template (suggested structure)

Each post should follow this skeleton when written:

```
1. Hook (50-100 words)
   — Specific scene the persona recognizes
   — Use a Hook from MESSAGING §2 if one fits

2. The problem framed (200-300 words)
   — Persona-specific pain
   — Why existing solutions fail
   — Anchor to evidence (research, beta data, competitor weakness)

3. The principle (200-300 words)
   — One of the 3 Pillars from BUFF_VALUES.md
   — Or one of the 8 territories BUFF owns from COMPETITORS §2

4. How BUFF embodies it (150-250 words)
   — 1-2 specific features
   — Soft, not pushy — this is a blog, not an ad

5. Practical takeaway for the reader (100-200 words)
   — What they can DO this week, with or without BUFF
   — Per BRAND §6: "give value before asking for any"

6. Call-to-action (50-100 words)
   — Newsletter signup OR free tier OR /philosophy link
   — Soft. One CTA per post.
```

**Total target length:** 800–1,400 words per post. Long enough to rank for SEO, short enough to actually be read.

## 8. SEO Requirements per Post

Each post must have:

- [ ] **Target keyword** in: title, first 100 words, one H2, meta description, image alt-text
- [ ] **Internal links** — 2–3 to other blog posts + 1 to product page (`/philosophy`, `/founding-100`, etc.)
- [ ] **External links** — 1–2 to authoritative sources (ADDitude, CHADD, peer-reviewed studies). Adds trust signal to Google.
- [ ] **Meta description** — ~150–160 chars, persona-targeted, includes target keyword
- [ ] **Featured image** — alt text includes target keyword + persona context
- [ ] **Open Graph tags** — auto-generated from frontmatter if blog infra supports
- [ ] **Schema.org `Article`** markup
- [ ] **Author bio block** — Adi's name + role + LinkedIn link (founder voice = trust signal)
