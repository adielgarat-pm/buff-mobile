## 3. Forum Reply Templates

### מיפוי: persona × scenario → template

טבלת ניווט מהירה:

| תרחיש בפורום | פרסונה דובר | תבנית |

| "I want something coach-aligned" | P5 | T7 |
| "Is it free?" | All | T8 |
| "How is it different from Joon?" | P3 | T9 |
| "Will my kid game the system?" | P3 | T10 |

### תבניות מלאות

**T1 — "Tried Joon, kid lost interest after a month":**
> *"That's the dopamine pattern with virtual rewards — they habituate fast. The fix that actually works is tying tasks to real-life rewards your kid chose: the concert ticket, the new gaming chair, the trip to ComicCon. BUFF works that way — kids see exactly how many days until their next real reward. We follow ages 6–18, so it's not a temporary fix."*

**T2 — "My teen says ADHD apps are babyish":**
> *"They're right about most of them. Joon caps at 12, Goally at 8, and most of the rest have a buddy character that 13yos hate. BUFF has a separate teen mode — no buddy, dashboard-style, dark theme — that was co-designed by a 15yo with ADHD. Worth showing your teen the actual screenshots before deciding."*

**T3 — "8yo just diagnosed, where do I start":**
> *"First: you're already doing the most important thing by reading and asking. For day-to-day, look for an app built on three things — (1) positive reinforcement only, no punishment mechanics; (2) real rewards your kid chooses, not virtual coins; (3) explicit goal of building independence so they outgrow the app. We built BUFF around all three. There's a free tier. The first week is just observing — no pressure on the kid."*

**T4 — "Burned out on sticker charts":**
> *"Sticker charts work for ~3 weeks then collapse the first time life disrupts them (illness, vacation, exam week). BUFF has a Pause Mode — one button, no progress lost, comes back with a 'Welcome back' instead of starting over. That alone solves the most common failure mode."*

**T5 — "Will it spy on my kid?":**
> *"No. Explicit policy: no location tracking, no behavior surveillance beyond task completion. Parents see completions and progress, nothing else. Per the product principles (we publish them): BUFF is a coaching tool, not a surveillance tool."*

**T6 — "What about teens?":**
> *"This is the gap I felt most acutely. Joon stops at 12, Tiimo is for adults, nothing in between for teens. BUFF has a dedicated Gamer Mode — no buddy character, dashboard-based, the kid proposes their own tasks and rewards. The parent doesn't dictate; they negotiate. Co-designed with a 15yo with ADHD."*

**T7 — "I want something that aligns with positive discipline":**
> *"BUFF was built around three explicit principles: intrinsic motivation (not extrinsic coins), positive coaching only (no punishment, no sad buddy, no streak penalties), and independence-building (kids outgrow the app). The pillars are public — they're in the product docs. If it ever drifts from them, that's a bug, not a feature."*

**T8 — "Is it free?":**
> *"Yes — free tier supports 1 child and 5 tasks, which is enough to test if it works for your family. Family plan ($9/mo) is for 3 kids, unlimited tasks. No ads ever — the audience is parents of ADHD kids under stress, ads would destroy trust."*
> **⚠ FLAG:** verify against PRD §5 + current grace period status before quoting prices in the wild.

**T9 — "How is BUFF different from Joon?":**
> *"Three big differences. (1) Rewards: Joon is virtual pet coins, BUFF is real-life rewards your kid chose. (2) Age range: Joon stops at 12, BUFF goes 6–18 with a teen mode. (3) Goal: Joon wants engagement, BUFF wants outgrowing. If your kid is under 10 and you want quick gamified fun, Joon is fine. For longer-term independence-building, BUFF."*

**T10 — "Won't my smart kid game the system?":**
> *"They can't self-issue BUFFs — parent approves each task completion. And the goal isn't to prevent gaming, it's to channel their cleverness toward proposing their own tasks and rewards. Kids who 'game' systems are usually under-stimulated by the system. BUFF lets them propose."*

## 4.5 Paywall Copy — by Parent's Stated Pain (AHA-triggered)

הפיירוול נורה אחרי שה-AHA event מזוהה (ראי [BUFF_BRAND.md §4](BUFF_BRAND.md)). ה-copy חייב להתייחס ל-*specific trophy task* שהילד הרגע השלים — לא ל-generic outcome. השם של הילד וה-task הספציפי מוטמעים דינמית.

### תבניות לפי קטגוריית כאב

**Pain category 1: Morning chaos**
> *"This morning, {kid_name} got ready for school before you had to ask. Not a small thing. That's the change you came here for — and you've earned the chance to keep it going."*
> CTA: *"Continue with Family Plan — $9/mo"*

**Pain category 2: Bedtime battles**
> *"Last night, {kid_name} started the bedtime routine themselves. You didn't have to negotiate. That moment is why you signed up — keep building it."*
> CTA: *"Continue with Family Plan — $9/mo"*

**Pain category 3: Bag / forgetting things** *(the trophy moment — most universal)*
> *"Last night, {kid_name} packed their bag for tomorrow — without you asking. That hasn't been a small thing in your house. It's exactly the moment you signed up for."*
> CTA: *"Continue with Family Plan — $9/mo"*

**Pain category 4: Homework wars**
> *"Today, {kid_name} started homework on their own. No reminders, no argument. That's the version of after-school you came here for."*
> CTA: *"Continue with Family Plan — $9/mo"*

**Pain category 5: General independence (fallback or "all of it")**
> *"This week, {kid_name} completed {N} routine tasks without you reminding them. The pattern you came here for is starting. Keep it going."*
> CTA: *"Continue with Family Plan — $9/mo"*

### תבנית "Preliminary AHA" — אם ה-trophy event לא נורה ב-12 הימים הראשונים

> *"You're 12 days in. {kid_name} has hit 70% completion {N} days in a row. That's the foundation that turns into the unprompted moments. Keep going — your subscription helps lock in the system."*
> CTA: *"Continue with Family Plan — $9/mo"*

### עברית

תבניות מקבילות בעברית לקופי הישראלי. עיקרון זהה — שם הילד + ה-task הספציפי + ה-acknowledgment של ה-pain שההורה ציינה ב-onboarding.

**Bag-packing (HE):**
> *"אתמול בלילה, {kid_name} ארז את התיק למחר — בלי שביקשת. זה לא דבר קטן בבית שלך. זה הרגע שבגללו נרשמת."*
> CTA: *"להמשיך עם Family Plan — $9 לחודש"*

### Anti-patterns ב-paywall copy

- ❌ *"Loved your free trial?"* — generic, doesn't reference what actually changed
- ❌ *"Unlock all features"* — features-first framing kills the emotional moment
- ❌ *"Your child is making progress!"* — vague, doesn't anchor to a SPECIFIC event
- ❌ Paywall fires more than 24 hours after AHA — emotional moment cools, conversion drops
- ❌ Paywall fires WITHOUT specific task reference — defeats the entire pain-targeting strategy

### Engineering requirements (out of MVP scope, on the list)

1. `is_pain_target` flag per task (set by onboarding answer + task category mapping)
2. `was_prompted` flag per task completion (true if reminder fired within X hours before completion)
3. AHA event listener — fires when `is_pain_target=true AND was_prompted=false AND completed=true` (first occurrence per family)
4. Push notification to parent within 2-4 hours of AHA event (with the trophy framing)
5. Paywall trigger 24 hours after AHA, with task name + kid name interpolated into copy

## 6. Reels / Shorts Script Templates

חמש תבניות שעובדות מוכחות לאפליקציות התנהגותיות. כל תבנית כוללת: hook (3 sec), setup (10 sec), payoff (5 sec), CTA (2 sec) = ~20 sec total.

> **קרא לפני שכותבים Reel חדש:** [BUFF_COMPETITORS.md §3.9](BUFF_COMPETITORS.md) — Category Creative Execution Patterns (live 2026-05-30 research). מסכם דפוסים רוויים מול ריקים, UGC formula, tonal benchmark מ-Calm/Headspace/BetterHelp, ו-format recs ספציפיים (length, hook style, person-on-camera, CTA pattern).

### Format 1: "POV: Morning Chaos → BUFF Reveal"

**Persona:** P1 Exhausted Morning
**Hook (00:00-00:03):** Text on screen: *"POV: It's 7:18am again."* Footage: phone showing 7:18, mom looking exhausted in bathroom mirror.
**Setup (00:03-00:13):** Quick cuts of past attempts — sticker chart falling off fridge, mom yelling "GET DRESSED" through closed door, kid distracted by Lego on the way to brush teeth.
**Payoff (00:13-00:18):** Screen swipe → BUFF dashboard. Kid checking off task himself. Quiet kitchen. Mom drinks coffee.
**CTA (00:18-00:20):** Text: *"From battles to wins. Try BUFF free."*

### Format 2: "Things I Stopped Saying" (carousel-style for Reel)

**Persona:** P1, P4
**Format:** Text-on-screen carousel, single shot of mom looking at camera neutrally.
**Slides (each 2-3 sec):**
1. *"Things I stopped saying after using BUFF for 3 months:"*
2. *"'Did you brush your teeth?'"*
3. *"'Are you ready for school?'"*
4. *"'How many times do I have to tell you?'"*
5. *"'Why is your bag still by the door?'"*
6. *"Now I just ask: 'How was your day?'"*
7. *"BUFF — until they don't need us."*

### Format 3: "Kid Testimonial" (Itay-style, age-authentic)

**Persona:** Targets P4 Teen-Lost-Control parents; child speaker C3 Itay-type
**Hook (00:00-00:03):** Teen at desk, headphones around neck, looking at camera. *"My mom downloaded another ADHD app. I told her this one is different."*
**Setup (00:03-00:13):** Cuts of teen using BUFF — proposing his own task ("study 30 min before gaming"), parent approving, teen checking it off on dashboard.
**Payoff (00:13-00:18):** Teen: *"I'm not being managed. I'm just running my own stuff."*
**CTA (00:18-00:20):** *"BUFF — Coach mode, not cop mode. Try the teen mode free."*

### Format 4: "Parent Confession" (text on screen, no face)

**Persona:** P1, P4
**Hook (00:00-00:03):** Black screen, white text: *"I'm a mom of a kid with ADHD. Here's what nobody told me."*
**Setup (00:03-00:13):** Hand-held shots — coffee, calendar, scattered toys. Text overlays:
- *"It's not about discipline."*
- *"It's not about screen time."*
- *"It's about the gap between knowing what to do and doing it."*
- *"And it's not their fault."*
**Payoff (00:13-00:18):** *"BUFF doesn't fix that gap. It just builds a bridge across it."*
**CTA (00:18-00:20):** *"Real rewards. Real ownership. Real growth. Free to try."*

### Format 5: "Before / After Day Comparison"

**Persona:** P1
**Hook (00:00-00:03):** Split screen — left labeled "Before BUFF", right "After BUFF". Both show 7:00am.
**Setup (00:03-00:13):** Split screen continues —
- LEFT: mom yelling at door, kid distracted, lost bag, frantic
- RIGHT: kid checking task off on phone, putting bag by door, mom reading paper
**Payoff (00:13-00:18):** Both screens at 7:25am. LEFT: chaos. RIGHT: in the car, calm.
**CTA (00:18-00:20):** *"Same family. Different morning. BUFF — free to start."*

### 7.4 Negative prompt (for all AI tools)

```
NEVER include in BUFF visuals:
- Cartoon pets, dragons, monsters, eggs hatching, virtual creatures
- Rocket ships, blast-offs, launches, countdowns with red timers
- Sticker-chart aesthetic, primary-color bursts, comic-book panels
- Drop shadows, heavy gradients, glossy 3D buttons
- Stock photos of perfectly-smiling families
- Children depicted as "broken" or "naughty"
- Behavior-chart imagery
- Police/cop/jail imagery (we say "not a cop" — don't visualize cops)
- Surveillance imagery (cameras, eyes, monitoring)
- Emojis other than the system ones (BUFFs, Vibe Check faces)
```

## 8. Channel-Specific Guidelines

### 8.1 Facebook ADHD Parenting Groups

**Tone:** Peer, not promoter. אם הקופי נקרא כמו ad — חוסם פעולה.
**Format:** Reply to a question (Templates §3), not unsolicited post.
**Founder voice:** Adi can post as founder ("I'm the founder of BUFF, but more importantly mom of a teen with ADHD…"). Disclosure builds trust.
**Frequency:** Max 2-3 replies/week per group. More = spammer flag.

### 8.2 Reddit (r/ADHD_parenting, r/ADHD)

**Tone:** Skeptical-tolerant. They smell hype 5 miles away.
**Format:** Long-form replies (200+ words) outperform short. Specific > vague.
**Founder voice:** OK to mention "we built X" but lead with the answer to their question.
**Rules:** Read each sub's rules — some ban founders entirely. r/ADHD specifically: be careful.
**Frequency:** Quality > quantity. One excellent reply > 10 short ones.

### 8.3 Instagram (Reels + Stories)

**Tone:** Visual, emotional, short.
**Format:** Reels (Format 1, 4) outperform static posts.
**Founder voice:** Adi-as-founder shorts work well **for warm/retargeted audiences**. **For cold acquisition — use UGC parent voice or no-face/back-of-head** (BetterHelp + Joon-clinician data via [COMPETITORS §3.9](BUFF_COMPETITORS.md) research). Itay-as-co-designer would work *spectacularly* if he's willing.
**Frequency:** 3-4 Reels/week to build feed signal.

**Tactical specs (from 2026-05-30 competitive research — see [COMPETITORS §3.9](BUFF_COMPETITORS.md)):**
- **Paid Reel length:** 22-25s (UGC ad-spy data: 15-25s is highest-survival window; need 22+ for Acknowledge→Reframe→Activate arc).
- **Organic Reel length:** 30-60s OK (room for reframe arc).
- **Hook (0-2s):** on-screen text reframe, **no logo, no UI, no question to viewer**. Statement. Examples from category: *"Homework can look like defiance from the outside"* (Savvy Kid). For BUFF: *"Your ADHD kid isn't the problem."*
- **Child on screen:** **never face**. Back-of-head, hands, or BUDDY avatar overlay.
- **CTA:** *"Comment BUDDY for the link"* (DM funnel, beats link-in-bio per Goally pattern).
- **Must read muted:** 50% of Reels scrolled muted — every beat needs on-screen text.
- **Visual differentiation:** BUFF's dark canvas + neon green = open lane vs. Joon's primary-color cartoon and Goally's tablet-hardware shots.
- **Stat to own:** Dr. William Dodson's *"20,000 corrections by age 10, 15:1 negative-to-positive ratio"* — verified empty lane in paid creative.

### 8.4 TikTok (post-MVP launch)

**Tone:** Vulnerable, specific, raw.
**Format:** Format 4 (Parent Confession) works best.
**Frequency:** Daily during launch month, then 4-5/week.

### 8.5 Google Play Store (key acquisition channel)

**Tone:** Direct, scannable, keyword-rich (but not stuffed).
**Format:** Use §5 copy verbatim.
**Update:** Every 2-3 months refresh promotional text + screenshots.

### 8.6 Email / Newsletter (post-signup)

**Tone:** Personal-letter from founder. Adi's voice.
**Format:** Plain text > HTML for first email. *"Hi from Adi — here's why I built BUFF"* etc.
**Frequency:** Weekly during onboarding (first 4 weeks), monthly thereafter.
