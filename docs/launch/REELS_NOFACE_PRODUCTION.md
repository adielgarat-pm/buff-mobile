## Shared production spec (no-face)
- **Shot on a phone**, vertical 9:16. No studio, no faces. B-roll only: hands, backpack, toothbrush, clock, coffee, app screen-recording, back-of-head OK.
- **Palette:** indigo `#1a1636` canvas · purple `#8b5cf6` accent · white text · soft lavender-grey secondary. Color-grade footage slightly cool/purple to match.
- **Captions on every beat** (50% watch muted). On-screen text given below in HE + EN — ship one language per cut; run both in parallel (HE warm circle, EN market).
- **Length:** organic 30–45s · paid cut 22–25s.
- **Hook = a statement in the first 3s.** No logo, no UI, no question to the viewer.
- **Music:** calm, warm, low — not upbeat "app ad" music. CapCut free library ("emotional/ambient").
- **Real features only** shown in screen-recs: child checking off own task · The Shop · Rest Tickets · Pause Mode. Nothing else.
- **CTA options:** (a) `Free on Google Play →` + landing link (see LAUNCH_KIT §3); (b) **"Comment BUDDY and I'll DM you the link"** (DM funnel beats link-in-bio for this category). One per platform.
- **Voice rules:** coach not cop · "real rewards" · "outgrow" · never streak/70%/lazy(except the negated hook)/disorder/cop-imagery.

## SCRIPT B — "Things I Stopped Saying"
**Emotional job:** relief + outcome ("my mornings changed"). **Moat:** parent stops being the cop; kid runs their own day. **Format:** text-on-screen list over calm domestic b-roll, no VO needed.

| 0:03–0:05 | *"'צחצחת שיניים?'"* / *"'Did you brush your teeth?'"* | Toothbrush on sink. Text styled struck-through / dissolving as it leaves. |

| 0:07–0:09 | *"'כמה פעמים אני צריכה להגיד לך?'"* / *"'How many times do I have to tell you?'"* | Shoes scattered in hall. |
| 0:09–0:11 | *"'למה התיק עדיין ליד הדלת?'"* / *"'Why is your bag still by the door?'"* | Hand reaching past the bag, leaving. |
| 0:11–0:18 **TURN** | *"עכשיו אני פשוט שואלת:"* → *"'איך היה היום שלך?'"* / *"Now I just ask:"* → *"'How was your day?'"* | Screen-rec (purple UI): a child's hand taps and checks off their OWN task. Then the backpack — now picked up, by the door, ready. |

**Why it works:** pattern-interrupt list = high shareability (Finch's relatable-moment formula); leads with the parent's felt experience, not a mechanic (our WHY rule); the only feature shown is real (child self-completes); no face → trivial to shoot.

## SCRIPT C — "Your Kid Isn't Lazy"
**Emotional job:** being seen / understood ("someone finally gets my kid"). **Moat:** we understand the ADHD brain — the lane Joon never touches. **Format:** text-on-screen + intimate b-roll, optional calm VO. Copy adapted from the brand-approved MESSAGING Format 4.

| Time | On-screen text (HE / EN) | Visual (no faces) |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:03 **HOOK** | *"הילד שלך לא עצלן."* / *"Your kid isn't lazy."* | Black-indigo, white text. Cut to: a kid's hand hovering over an open backpack / homework — not starting. |
| 0:03–0:07 | *"זה לא עניין של משמעת. זה לא כוח רצון."* / *"It's not about discipline. It's not about willpower."* | Slow b-roll: pencil untouched, page blank, fidget. |
| 0:07–0:12 | *"זה הפער בין לדעת מה צריך לעשות — לבין להתחיל."* / *"It's the gap between knowing what to do — and starting."* | Clock ticking, hand still hovering. |
| 0:12–0:15 | *"וזו לא אשמתו."* / *"And it's not their fault."* | A breath of stillness. White text on indigo. |
| 0:15–0:20 **BRIDGE** | *"BUFF לא 'מתקן' את הפער. הוא בונה גשר מעליו — צעד ראשון קטן מספיק."* / *"BUFF doesn't 'fix' the gap. It builds a bridge across it — a first step small enough to take."* | Screen-rec (purple UI): one small task on the board; the hand finally taps it. |
| 0:20–0:24 **CTA** | *"מאמן, לא שוטר. נסי את BUFF — חינם."* / *"A coach, not a cop. Try BUFF — free."* | Purple end-card, wordmark, CTA. |

**Why it works:** "being seen" converts mothers (parent-ad research); the hook speaks the parent's secret fear and flips it; reuses brand-approved language ("and it's not their fault"); the bridge shows a real action (a small task), no vapor feature; no face.

## How to run it (the Finch model — zero budget)
1. **One strong account**, not many. Finch grew to $30M ARR organically off one TikTok account + relatable, emotional content. Post both scripts as organic Reels first; let engagement tell you the winner before spending a shekel.
2. **Test B vs C** — different emotional jobs. Whichever earns more saves/shares/comments becomes the template for the next 3–4.
3. **Generous free tier in the message** — Finch's soft paywall let people fully feel the app first. Our CTA says "free," not "trial."
4. **DM-funnel CTA** ("Comment BUDDY") for cold organic; landing link for warm/retargeted.

> ⚠️ Verify any price/claim against current PRD before a public cut. Child never on camera. Captions mandatory.
