# BUFF — AI Video Prompts for the 3 No-Face Reels

> Generate the b-roll with AI instead of shooting it. **No faces = ideal for AI** (no uncanny-valley, no character consistency problems). Each prompt = one ~5s clip. Add on-screen text + end-card in CapCut afterward (NOT in the AI gen).
> Scripts: `REELS_HOOK_TEST_EN.md` · Shot map: `REELS_SHOTLIST.md`.

## Which tool (zero budget, June 2026)
- **Kling AI** — generous free credits, strong realism, great **image-to-video**. Best starting point.
- **Google Veo (via Gemini/Flow)** — top quality if you have access.
- **Runway / Pika** — free trial credits, good for short b-roll.
- **Workflow that gives the most control:** generate a **still image** you like first (Midjourney / DALL·E / or a Canva frame I make for you), then use the tool's **image-to-video** to animate it. More consistent and on-brand than pure text-to-video.

## Global style line (paste into every prompt)
> *Cinematic vertical 9:16 b-roll. Warm natural domestic morning light, slightly cool and muted color grade. Shallow depth of field, gentle slow camera move. Realistic, intimate, calm. NO people's faces, NO text, NO logos, NO captions, NO cartoon elements. Documentary realism.*
