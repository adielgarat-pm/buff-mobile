# BUFF — Hook A/B Test, 3 No-Face Reels (English)

> Three short, ready-to-shoot scripts that test three different opening hooks against each other. **No faces.** Same format and mostly the same b-roll — shoot all three in one session, post organically, let 48–72h of engagement pick the winner (the Finch model). Then double down on the winner.
>
> Research basis: hook wins or loses in the first 3s · UGC mobile-shot > polished · parent-emotion is Joon's open lane · "being seen" converts mothers · lead with feeling, not mechanics.

---

## Shared spec (read once)
- **Phone-shot, vertical 9:16. No faces.** B-roll only: hands, backpack, toothbrush, clock, coffee, scattered shoes, app screen-recording, back-of-head OK.
- **Palette:** indigo `#1a1636` bg · purple `#8b5cf6` accent · white headline text · lavender-grey secondary. Grade footage slightly cool/purple.
- **Captions on every beat** — big, white, readable muted. The hook is on screen at 0:00.
- **Length:** ~24s (paid-cut safe; extend b-roll to 35–40s for organic).
- **Music:** calm, warm, low. Not upbeat "app-ad" music.
- **Real features only** in screen-recs: child checking off their own task · The Shop (real-life reward the kid picks) · Rest Tickets · Pause Mode. Nothing else.
- **CTA (cold organic):** "Comment BUDDY and I'll DM you the link." **(warm/retargeted):** "Free on Google Play →" + landing link.
- **End-card (identical all 3):** indigo, BUFF wordmark, purple tagline *"until they don't need us."*

## Test plan (zero budget)
1. Post all three as **organic** Reels (Instagram + the warm FB/WhatsApp circle), same day, same end-card.
2. Watch **48–72h**: rank by saves + shares + comments (not just views — saves/shares predict intent).
3. The winning **hook** becomes the template; reshoot the next 3 Reels as variations on it.
4. Keep the free-tier message explicit ("free," not "trial") — Finch's generous-free-tier framing drove organic word-of-mouth.

> ⚠️ Verify any price/claim against current PRD before a public cut. No child faces. Captions mandatory. Hook on screen at 0:00.
