# BUFF — Reels Scripts (June 2026 launch)

> Rewrite of 5 NotebookLM concepts. **Every script uses only SHIPPED features** (verified against `src/` 2026-06-16), in the **parent-emotional** register for cold acquisition, in the **purple-indigo** palette.
>
> **Dropped from the NotebookLM drafts (don't exist in the app):** Visible Timer · Cognitive Strategy Library · Focus Buffs / Checklist / First Step · Energy Buffs.
> **Banned by brand voice:** "Streak", "70%" (mechanic-led + under revision). Use the *feeling*, name the mechanic only as proof.
> **Real features we CAN show:** BUFFs ⚡ · **The Shop** (real-life rewards the kid chooses) · **Rest Tickets** · **Pause Mode** · parent-plans / child-executes (two interfaces) · teen mode co-designed by a teen.

---

## Shared production spec

- **Palette:** indigo canvas `#1a1636` · purple accent `#8b5cf6` · white text · ONE high-contrast CTA. (Matches the real app, so screen-recordings look authentic.)
- **Length:** organic 30–60s · paid/ad cut 22–25s.
- **Captions mandatory** — 50% watch muted; every beat needs on-screen text (given below in HE + EN).
- **Child on screen: never the face.** Back-of-head, hands, or app screen-recording only.
- **Tool:** edit real phone footage in **CapCut** (free). App-reveal / end-card → Canva in BUFF palette.
- **CTA options:** (a) landing page link; (b) **"Comment BUDDY and I'll DM you the link"** — DM funnel beats link-in-bio for this category. Pick one per platform.
- **Voice rules:** Coach not cop · "real rewards" not coins · "Pause/Resume" not stop · "outgrow" not quit.

## Script 1 — "Not lazy. Just can't start." (task initiation)
**Audience:** organic / authority-building · **Format:** Parent confession, no face · **Real feature:** none shown — pure psychoeducation + brand outro (the Visible Timer it originally leaned on does NOT exist).

| Beat | On-screen text (HE / EN) | Direction |
|---|---|---|
| Hook 0–3s | *"חשבתי שהילד שלי עצלן." / "I thought my kid was lazy."* | Black-indigo screen, white text. No footage yet. |
| 3–13s | *"הוא לא. פשוט אצל מוח ADHD — ההתחלה היא החלק הכי קשה." / "He's not. With an ADHD brain, starting is the hardest part."* | Hand-held: backpack untouched, kid's hand hovering, not starting. Warm domestic. |
| 13–18s | *"לא צריך כוח רצון. צריך צעד ראשון קטן מספיק." / "It's not willpower. It's a first step small enough to take."* | Single tiny task tapped on the app (screen-rec, purple UI). |
| CTA 18–22s | *"BUFF — מאמן, לא שוטר." / "BUFF — a coach, not a cop."* + tagline *until they don't need us* | Purple end-card, app icon. |
