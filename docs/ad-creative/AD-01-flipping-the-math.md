# BUFF Ad #1 — "Flipping the Math"

> The first paid-social Reel for BUFF.
> Status: production-ready draft awaiting Adi sign-off on a small set of open decisions (§11).
>
> **Strategic basis:** [BUFF_COMPETITORS.md §3.9](../BUFF_COMPETITORS.md) Category Creative Execution Patterns (live 2026-05-30 research) + [BUFF_VALUES.md](../BUFF_VALUES.md) Values Check + [BUFF_PERSONAS.md](../BUFF_PERSONAS.md) emotional jobs (P1 + P2 + P5).
>
> **Concept (one line):** Plant the Dodson "20,000 corrections by age 10" stat in the parent's mind. Reframe both parent and kid as fighting an asymmetric math problem — not a parenting failure, not a broken kid. Position BUFF as the equalizer.

## 1. Why v2 (not the rejected v1)

**v1 opened with parent shame** (*"this morning I screamed at my kid in the parking lot"*) — rejected by Adi as off-brand. Reasons captured for the record:

- ❌ **Anti-Pillar-2** (Positive Coaching): if BUFF doesn't shame kids inside the product, it must not shame parents in marketing. Internal consistency = brand trust.
- ❌ **Category misalignment:** no direct ADHD competitor uses shame-led copy in 2026 (Joon = product-utility; Goally = hardware-safety; Savvy Kid = explicit anti-yelling; Brili = founder-warmth; Tiimo = neurodivergent-safe). Shame-led would have been a brand-misaligned outlier.
- ❌ **Long-term brand damage:** a parent who clicks because of guilt remembers BUFF as the brand that made them feel bad. We compound trust, not extract it.

**v2 uses the same Dodson stat — as fuel for empathy, not as a weapon.**

## 4. Voiceover script (verbatim, English)

```
[soft pad enters at 0:03]

By age ten, ADHD kids hear about twenty thousand corrections.

Fifteen negatives. For every one positive.

[music lifts slightly at 0:11]

BUFF flips the math.

Daily wins. Real progress.

[music thins out at 0:16]

A coaching tool. Not a surveillance tool.

[music fades to subtle sting at 0:20]

Comment BUDDY for the link.
```

**Voice direction:**
- Soft female parent voice. Calm. **Not announcer. Not breathy.**
- Slow on *"twenty thousand"* — give it weight.
- Crisper on *"Daily wins"* — pivot energy.
- Total VO runtime: ~17 seconds across the 24s spot. Silent space is OK — text carries the muted 50%.
