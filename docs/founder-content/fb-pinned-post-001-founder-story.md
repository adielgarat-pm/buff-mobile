## English (primary)

```
Why I'm building an app my own kid is supposed to outgrow

For ten years I built digital products for a living. Then I tried to fix
mornings with my own son, who has ADHD — and every app we tried lasted
about six weeks.

When I showed him my first prototype — points, streaks, a cute pet that
grows when you complete tasks — he looked at it for 30 seconds and asked:

"What happens if I miss a day?"
"You lose your streak."
"That's stressful. I don't want that."

He was 12. He was right.

So we built BUFF differently — together. He's 15 now, and he co-designed
the teen side himself.

🟣 Real-life rewards your kid actually wants — not virtual coins
🟣 70% of a routine done = a win, not a failure
🟣 A Pause button for sick days and hard weeks, guilt-free
🟣 Built for ages 6–18 — and built to be outgrown. If your kid stops
   needing it, that's the success.

BUFF launches soon on Google Play. Until then I'm opening 100 Founding
Member spots — lifetime access for families who want in early and help
shape it.

Curious? Comment or DM me and I'll send the link. And if you just want to
swap ADHD-morning war stories — that's welcome too.

— Adi
```

## Posting notes

1. **Link goes in the first comment, not the body** — "Comment or DM me" converts
   better on a personal post than a bare link, and matches the DM-for-invite
   mechanic observed in Track G. Drop `buffadhd.com` (or `/founding-100` once live)
   as the first comment after publishing.
2. **Pin it:** publish → ⋯ on the post → "Pin to profile". Pinned + Featured link
   together complete the profile-as-landing-page funnel (H-1).
3. **The dialog stays verbatim** — it is the emotional center that earns the rest
   (same note as the LinkedIn essay). Everything else: adapt to your voice.
4. **Privacy:** son referenced as "my 15-year-old", no name required (naming is
   allowed per FOUNDER_STORY §3.2 — Adi's call). Emi is not mentioned
   (privacy-gated).
5. Brand Check: no failure language, no pressure framing, no lightning-bolt emoji
   (BRAND §7.7 — bolt lives only in the logo), coach-not-cop voice throughout.
